The Experience

From photo to fix — in seconds

Flora turns a single photo into expert agricultural advice. No forms, no waiting.

01

Snap & Upload

Take a clear photo of your plant or affected leaf and drop it into Flora's chat. Any device, any angle.

02

AI Reads the Image

Flora's vision AI scans every detail — leaf texture, color patterns, spots — and identifies what's happening with your plant.

03

Instant Smart Diagnosis

In seconds, Flora generates a full diagnosis: disease name, symptoms, root cause, and confidence level — streamed live to your screen.

04

Actionable Treatment Plan

Get step-by-step treatment instructions you can apply immediately. Ask follow-up questions and Flora remembers the full context.

05

Saved for Later

Every diagnosis and conversation is automatically saved. Track your plant's health journey over time and refer back anytime.
